Menu-ready tomatoes

Tomatoes for prep lines, salads, sauces, and specials

Tomatoes move through almost every restaurant station: salad, sandwiches, prep, sauces, breakfast, bar garnish, catering, and seasonal specials. Garden Fresh helps chefs plan the right mix of slicing tomatoes, grape tomatoes, roma tomatoes, cherry tomatoes, and specialty varieties.

Our Pompano Beach team supports Broward and Palm Beach restaurants with refrigerated delivery, standing orders, and practical ordering support when volume or menus change. Use weekly pars for staples and add specialty tomatoes when seasonal menus call for them.

For kitchens that care about ripeness, send the use case and receiving window. We will help align product to immediate prep or later-week use when availability allows.

  • Common requests include beefsteak, roma, grape, cherry, heirloom, and slicing tomatoes.

Tomato FAQ

Restaurant ordering questions

What tomato varieties can restaurants request?

Restaurants can request beefsteak, roma, grape, cherry, heirloom, slicing, and specialty tomatoes based on season and availability.

Can tomatoes be planned by ripeness?

Yes. Garden Fresh can help restaurants plan ready-to-use tomatoes and firmer product for later prep when availability allows.

Do you deliver tomatoes to restaurants in Broward and Palm Beach?

Yes. Garden Fresh delivers tomatoes and other wholesale produce on local restaurant routes across Broward and Palm Beach Counties.

Can tomatoes be included in standing orders?

Yes. High-use tomatoes can be added to weekly pars for salads, sandwiches, prep, sauces, bars, and specials.
