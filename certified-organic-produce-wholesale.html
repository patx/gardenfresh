Certified Organic FAQ

Questions chefs ask

Can restaurants request certified organic produce?

Yes. Restaurants can request certified organic produce options, including organic greens, fruit, herbs, roots, and specialty items when available.

Is organic produce available every day?

Organic availability changes by item, season, grower, and market conditions. We can confirm options during ordering.

Can organic items be mixed with conventional produce orders?

Yes. Restaurants can add organic items to a regular produce order guide alongside conventional staples and specialty requests.

Do you deliver organic produce in Broward and Palm Beach Counties?

Yes. Available organic produce can be delivered on local restaurant routes across Broward and Palm Beach Counties.
