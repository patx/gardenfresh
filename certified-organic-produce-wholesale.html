Organic produce sourcing

Organic options for restaurants that need cleaner menu sourcing

Restaurants often need certified organic produce for specific menu items, wellness programs, private chef clients, hotel offerings, school programs, or customer requests. We can help identify organic options across greens, fruit, herbs, roots, and specialty produce when available.

Organic produce availability moves by item, season, grower, and market conditions. Our team can confirm available organic items, discuss substitutions, and add approved items to your regular restaurant order guide.

Use organic items alongside conventional staples when a full organic program is not needed. This keeps ordering practical while allowing chefs to support specific menu promises and guest preferences.

  • Common requests include organic greens, herbs, berries, roots, citrus, fruit, and specialty produce.
  • Availability depends on item, season, grower supply, and order timing.
  • Organic items can be combined with regular restaurant produce orders for local delivery.

Organic produce FAQ

Restaurant ordering questions

Can restaurants request certified organic produce?

Yes. Restaurants can request certified organic produce options, including organic greens, fruit, herbs, roots, and specialty items when available.

Is organic produce available every day?

Organic availability changes by item, season, grower, and market conditions. We can confirm options during ordering.

Can organic items be mixed with conventional produce orders?

Yes. Restaurants can add organic items to a regular produce order guide alongside conventional staples and specialty requests.

Do you deliver organic produce in Broward and Palm Beach Counties?

Yes. Available organic produce can be delivered on local restaurant routes across Broward and Palm Beach Counties.
