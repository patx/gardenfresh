Trip-ready produce

Dockside provisioning with staged ripeness

Marine provisioning requires more than a standard produce drop. We plan by itinerary length, guest and crew count, storage space, departure marina, and expected usage so produce is packed in practical waves for the full trip.

  • Day-1 ready, mid-trip, and end-trip ripeness planning for bananas, avocados, tomatoes, mangos, and more.
  • Labeled crates, galley-friendly packs, split cases, trimmed greens, and herb bundles.
  • Cold-chain delivery to major South Florida marinas and private docks.
  • Early-AM windows, last-minute top-offs, and tournament-week support when available.
