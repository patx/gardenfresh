Chef-focused ordering

Fresh produce that fits daily restaurant prep

Restaurants are the core of Garden Fresh. We keep ordering practical for chefs and owners who need consistent quality, flexible pack sizes, and quick help when menus, reservations, weather, or weekend volume shift.

Use us for leafy greens, tomatoes, herbs, citrus, tropical fruit, berries, roots, mushrooms, organics, microgreens, edible flowers, and specialty produce. Full cases, split cases, and by-the-pound ordering help kitchens protect margins while keeping menus fresh.

Our local routes support restaurants across Broward and Palm Beach Counties, including waterfront dining rooms, country clubs, hotel kitchens, cafes, bars, catering teams, private chefs, and neighborhood restaurants.

  • Order by 12AM for next-day delivery on most restaurant produce items.
  • No order minimums, no delivery fees, no fuel charges, and free second runs when route capacity allows.
  • Standing orders keep staple prep items consistent while allowing seasonal specials and weekly changes.

Common restaurant ordering questions

What makes Garden Fresh different for restaurants?

Garden Fresh is built around chef workflows: split cases, standing orders, late cutoffs, no order minimums, no delivery fees, and local support from a Pompano Beach team.

Can restaurants build a custom order guide?

Yes. Send your prep staples, weekly volume, delivery window, and seasonal items. We can build a practical online order guide for your kitchen.

Do you deliver to both Broward and Palm Beach Counties?

Yes. Garden Fresh runs refrigerated restaurant produce routes across Broward and Palm Beach Counties, with city pages for major local service areas.

Can I order specialty produce for menu changes?

Yes. Restaurants can request specialty mushrooms, microgreens, edible flowers, tropical fruit, organics, and seasonal produce when available.
