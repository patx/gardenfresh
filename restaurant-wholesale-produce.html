How we support you

Restaurants: produce that fits the operation

Garden Fresh is built around how restaurant kitchens actually run: orders placed after service ends, trucks dispatched at 4 AM, product in the walk-in before prep starts. We supply restaurants, cafes, bars, caterers, and private chefs across Broward and Palm Beach Counties with the full produce range — staples through specialty.

Split cases and by-the-pound options keep food cost honest. Standing orders hold your pars steady. There are no order minimums, no delivery fees, and no fuel charges on local routes — and when a menu changes at 10 PM, the cutoff for most next-day items is 12 AM.

Service FAQ

Common questions

What makes Garden Fresh different for restaurants?

Garden Fresh is built around chef workflows: split cases, standing orders, late cutoffs, no order minimums, no delivery fees, and local support from a Pompano Beach team.

Can restaurants build a custom order guide?

Yes. Send your prep staples, weekly volume, delivery window, and seasonal items. We can build a practical online order guide for your kitchen.

Do you deliver to both Broward and Palm Beach Counties?

Yes. Garden Fresh runs refrigerated restaurant produce routes across Broward and Palm Beach Counties, with city pages for major local service areas.

Can I order specialty produce for menu changes?

Yes. Restaurants can request specialty mushrooms, microgreens, edible flowers, tropical fruit, organics, and seasonal produce when available.
