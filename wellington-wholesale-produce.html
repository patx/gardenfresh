Palm Beach County restaurant routes

Produce built for Wellington kitchens

Wellington's food economy changes with the horses. From January through April, the Winter Equestrian Festival and polo season turn the village into an international hospitality operation — VIP tents, sponsor pavilions, club dining rooms, and private chefs cooking for the sport's wealthiest owners — then the covers settle back to a polished suburban baseline around Wellington Green and the Forest Hill corridor.

Seasonal whiplash is a produce-supply problem, and it is one we plan for. Event caterers and club kitchens hold a baseline standing order and layer show-week volume on top by the midnight cutoff; specialty items — microgreens, edible flowers, tasting-menu produce — ride the same trucks as the staple cases. The run from Pompano Beach takes about forty-five minutes across the county.

  • Order by 12 AM for next-day delivery on most restaurant produce items.
  • Use standing orders for prep staples and update specials as menus change.
  • No order minimums, no delivery fees, and no fuel charges on local routes.

Wellington produce delivery questions

Can you handle Winter Equestrian Festival and polo season volume?

Yes. Event caterers and club kitchens layer show-week volume on a standing baseline by the midnight cutoff — season step-ups are planned into the Wellington route every winter.

Do you supply VIP hospitality and private chefs during show season?

Yes. Specialty and tasting-menu produce — microgreens, edible flowers, premium fruit — delivers alongside staple cases, with split cases so precise menus stay precise.

What is the cutoff for next-day delivery to Wellington?

Most produce orders can be placed by 12 AM for next-day delivery. We confirm timing based on route position, item availability, and your receiving window.
