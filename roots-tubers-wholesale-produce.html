Restaurants can request potatoes, sweet potatoes, yams, carrots, beets, onions, turnips, radishes, and other root vegetables based on availability.

Are roots and tubers good for standing orders?

Yes. Potatoes, onions, carrots, and other high-use staples work well as weekly standing order items.

Do you deliver root vegetables across Broward and Palm Beach?

Yes. Garden Fresh delivers root vegetables and other wholesale produce on local routes across Broward and Palm Beach Counties.

Can restaurants order roots with greens and herbs?

Yes. Root vegetables can be ordered with leafy greens, herbs, tomatoes, fruit, berries, organics, and specialty produce.
