Berries for restaurants, bakeries, hotels, and catering teams

Berries require careful handling and predictable ordering. Garden Fresh helps restaurants and hospitality kitchens plan strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, blackberries, and mixed berries for breakfast, pastry, brunch, desserts, salads, cocktails, and banquet service.

Local refrigerated routes across Broward and Palm Beach Counties help kitchens receive berries alongside citrus, tropical fruit, leafy greens, herbs, roots, organics, and specialty produce. Use standing pars for regular menus and add seasonal volume for events.

For delicate items, communicate expected usage and receiving windows so our team can help match availability to your service schedule.

  • Good fit for restaurants, hotel breakfast programs, bakeries, dessert menus, caterers, and clubs.

Restaurant ordering questions

What berries can restaurants order?

Restaurants can request strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, blackberries, mixed berries, and seasonal berry items based on availability.

Are berries delivered refrigerated?

Yes. Garden Fresh handles berry delivery through refrigerated produce routes for Broward and Palm Beach County accounts.

Can berries be used for hotels and bakeries?

Yes. Berries are commonly ordered by restaurants, hotels, bakeries, caterers, clubs, and dessert programs.

Can berries be ordered with citrus and tropical fruit?

Yes. Berries can be ordered with citrus, tropical fruit, leafy greens, herbs, roots, and specialty produce.
