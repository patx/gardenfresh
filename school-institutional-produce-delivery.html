Service FAQ

School and institutional produce questions

Can Garden Fresh support recurring institutional orders?

Yes. Garden Fresh can set standing orders for weekly menus, office programs, healthcare kitchens, and planned cafeteria usage.

Can you provide food safety documentation?

Yes. W-9, COI, and food safety documentation are available on request.

Do institutions need to meet an order minimum?

No. Local accounts have no order minimums, no delivery fees, and no fuel charges.

Can deliveries align with receiving windows?

Garden Fresh works with receiving windows and route timing whenever possible across Broward and Palm Beach Counties.
