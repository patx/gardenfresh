Specialty produce sourcing for chefs and seasonal menus

Specialty produce helps restaurants differentiate plates, add color, and support seasonal menus. Garden Fresh works with chefs across Broward and Palm Beach Counties to source microgreens, edible flowers, specialty mushrooms, exotic fruit, specialty herbs, baby vegetables, and limited items.

Because specialty produce changes quickly, the best orders include usage date, quantity, preferred pack, menu application, and substitution notes. Our local team can confirm availability and help add items to your regular route when timing allows.

Use specialty produce for chef tastings, tasting menus, private events, club dinners, seasonal specials, garnish, and premium hospitality programs.

  • Common requests include microgreens, edible flowers, specialty mushrooms, exotic fruit, baby vegetables, and specialty herbs.
  • Tell us menu use, delivery date, quantity, and substitution options so we can confirm availability.
  • Specialty items can often ride with regular restaurant produce deliveries.

Specialty produce FAQ

Restaurant ordering questions

What specialty produce can chefs request?

Chefs can request microgreens, edible flowers, specialty mushrooms, exotic fruit, specialty herbs, baby vegetables, and seasonal produce based on availability.

How should restaurants order specialty produce?

Send the menu use, needed date, quantity, pack preference, and substitution notes so We can confirm availability and timing.

Can specialty produce be delivered with regular orders?

Yes. Specialty produce can often be added to regular restaurant produce orders when available and ordered within route timing.

Do you source microgreens and edible flowers?

Yes. We can source microgreens, edible flowers, and similar chef-driven items based on availability.
