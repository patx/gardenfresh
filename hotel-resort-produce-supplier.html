Service FAQ

Hotel and resort produce questions

Can Garden Fresh support hotel standing orders?

Yes. Hotels and resorts can set pars for breakfast fruit, bar citrus, salads, banquets, and outlet staples, then adjust for occupancy or events.

Do you provide produce for banquets and events?

Yes. Garden Fresh supports planned events, top-offs, and specialty requests for banquets, clubs, and resort food and beverage teams.

Do hospitality accounts have delivery fees?

No. Local accounts receive no delivery fees, no fuel charges, and no order minimums.

Can you provide documentation for purchasing?

W-9, COI, and food safety documentation are available on request.
