Produce delivery FAQ

Straight answers about minimums, fees, cutoffs, routes, and account setup for South Florida foodservice kitchens.

Does Garden Fresh have order minimums?

No. Local accounts have no order minimums, so restaurants and foodservice teams can order around actual prep needs.

Does Garden Fresh charge delivery or fuel fees?

No. Garden Fresh offers no delivery fees and no fuel charges for local accounts in the service area.

What is the cutoff for next-day delivery?

Most produce orders can be placed by 12 AM for next-day delivery, depending on item availability and route timing.

Which counties do you serve?

Garden Fresh focuses on Broward County and Palm Beach County, with city route pages for major restaurant and foodservice areas.

Can restaurants order split cases?

Yes. Restaurants can order full cases, split cases, and select by-the-pound items to control waste and food cost.

Can Garden Fresh support standing orders?

Yes. Standing orders are available for weekly pars, staple produce, and planned menu needs.

Do you offer specialty produce?

Yes. Specialty produce may include microgreens, edible flowers, specialty mushrooms, tropical fruit, organics, and seasonal items.

How do new accounts get started?

Send your business name, delivery address, contact info, receiving window, and sample prep list through the contact form or by phone.
