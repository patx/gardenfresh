Route coverage

A local delivery footprint built from Pompano Beach

Garden Fresh is based in Pompano Beach, which gives the team a practical position for routes through Broward County and north into Palm Beach County. The delivery area pages below are written for restaurants and foodservice buyers who want a local supplier familiar with their part of South Florida.

Use the county and city links to find the page closest to your kitchen, then contact sales with your receiving window and weekly usage. Some special routes or out-of-area requests may depend on timing, order profile, and route capacity.

The route pages also help search engines understand how Garden Fresh serves South Florida at the county and city level. Each local page points buyers toward nearby routes, restaurant ordering resources, and direct account setup.

FAQ

Common questions

Does Garden Fresh serve all of Broward and Palm Beach Counties?

Garden Fresh focuses on major restaurant and foodservice routes across Broward and Palm Beach Counties. Some out-of-area or special routes may depend on timing and volume.

Where is Garden Fresh based?

Garden Fresh operates from Pompano Beach, which supports local delivery routes north and south across the two-county service area.

Are city pages only for restaurants?

City pages are restaurant-focused, but hotels, clubs, schools, institutions, caterers, and private chefs can also use Garden Fresh in those areas.

How do I confirm route availability?

Contact Garden Fresh with your delivery address, receiving window, and expected weekly usage to confirm route timing.
