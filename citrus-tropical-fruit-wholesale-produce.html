Bar and menu fruit

Citrus, tropical fruit, and ripeness planning for local kitchens

Citrus and tropical fruit are central to South Florida restaurants. Bars need dependable lemons, limes, oranges, and grapefruit; kitchens need fruit for breakfast, dessert, ceviche, garnish, sauces, salads, and seasonal plates.

We support Broward and Palm Beach restaurants with refrigerated delivery, standing orders, practical pack sizes, and communication around ripeness-sensitive items like bananas, avocados, mangos, and pineapple.

Whether you run a beachside restaurant, hotel bar, private club, caterer, or yacht provisioning kitchen, our team can help plan weekly fruit pars and specialty requests.

  • Common items include lemons, limes, oranges, grapefruit, mangos, pineapple, avocados, bananas, and seasonal tropicals.
  • Bar programs can build citrus into standing orders with weekly adjustment support.
  • Ripeness windows can be noted for bananas, avocados, mangos, and other sensitive fruit when available.
