Broward County restaurant routes

Produce built for Parkland kitchens

Parkland sits at Broward's quiet northwest edge — a low-density, high-expectation city where most professional cooking happens inside country club dining rooms and a handful of upscale plaza restaurants. Club members here eat like regulars because they are: the same faces at lunch after golf, the same families at Sunday dinner, and a member survey waiting for any kitchen that slips.

Member-grade consistency is a produce spec, not a slogan — same size, same trim, same ripeness, every case. Club chefs run standing orders against that spec and use split cases for wine dinners, member-guest tournaments, and holiday banquets. Parkland caps the northwest run out of Pompano Beach, about twenty-five minutes door to door.

  • Order by 12 AM for next-day delivery on most restaurant produce items.
  • Use standing orders for prep staples and update specials as menus change.
  • No order minimums, no delivery fees, and no fuel charges on local routes.

Local delivery FAQ

Parkland produce delivery questions

Can you hold consistent produce specs for Parkland club kitchens?

Yes. Standing orders lock a spec — size, trim, ripeness — and our team flags substitutions before they ship, not after they arrive.

Do you handle tournament and banquet volume for Parkland clubs?

Yes. Layer event volume on top of your standing baseline by the midnight cutoff — member-guest weekends and holiday banquets are routine adds.

Are there delivery fees, fuel charges, or order minimums?

No. We do not add delivery fees or fuel charges on local routes, and there is no order minimum for standard account setup.

Can Parkland kitchens order online?

Yes. Once your account is set up, you can browse the catalog and place orders online, or send orders by phone or text — whatever fits your workflow.
