Leafy greens FAQ

Restaurant ordering questions

What leafy greens can restaurants order from Garden Fresh?

Restaurants can request romaine, spring mix, spinach, kale, iceberg, arugula, mixed lettuces, and other salad greens based on availability.

Do you deliver leafy greens across Broward and Palm Beach Counties?

Yes. Garden Fresh delivers leafy greens on refrigerated restaurant routes across Broward and Palm Beach Counties.

Can restaurants order split cases of leafy greens?

Yes. Local accounts can use full cases, split cases, and practical pack sizes where available to match prep volume and reduce waste.

Can leafy greens be added to a standing restaurant order?

Yes. Romaine, spring mix, spinach, kale, and other high-use greens can be built into weekly pars and adjusted as menus change.
